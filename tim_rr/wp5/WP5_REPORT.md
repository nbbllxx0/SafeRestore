# WP5: tolerance sweep, native-acquisition arm and degradation-shift stress test (TIM-26-11920 R&R)

**Date:** 2026-10-25. **Scope:** WP5 of `records/2026-10-25-tim-rr-revision-plan.md` (rows R2.1 and
R2.4, evidence item E7), plus the WP2 prevalence flag (experiment D).

**Status label.** Every result here is **retrospective and descriptive**. All runs are inference-only
with the saved `detector.pt` / `restorer.pt` and the stored detector thresholds; nothing was retrained.
The KSDD2 tolerance radius (8 px) and the analysis adaptations were chosen with qualification-split
outcomes in view (`records/2026-10-25-tim-rr-wp1-provenance.md` §2). Experiment C lies **outside the
guarantee** by construction: it tests fixed gates on a population that differs from the qualified one.

## Headline

- **A (R2.4).** No tolerance radius from 0 to 16 px qualifies a KSDD2 portfolio (0/5 at every radius,
  with standard and with strict labels). The 8-px ring hides most boundary calls: on positive images,
  91% of the calls outside the mask fall inside the ring. If ring calls count as activation, the
  positive-image incidence is 0.48–0.49 at every radius.
- **B (R2.1).** Native-acquisition arm. Carinthia-S qualifies 5/5 with 81–100% test auto-acceptance.
  The raw evidence-loss rate is 0.000. This is consistent with the native image being the detector's
  training distribution (inferred: the detector was trained on reference images).
  KSDD2 qualifies 0/5 portfolios even without synthetic degradation: the raw native evidence-loss rate is
  0.251, above α = 0.15.
- **C (outside the guarantee).** Fixed Carinthia-S gates stay within 0.15 on both endpoints under motion
  blur and defocus in 5/5 seeds. Poisson–Gaussian noise breaks the loss endpoint in 1/5 portfolio
  selections and in 9/27 single-policy gates. JPEG breaks the gates. Acceptance collapses below 5% in
  10 of 15 seed-quality cases. In 9 of the 13 cases where the gate still accepted at least one image,
  activation was 15.6–91.1%; some of these rest on only 4–6 accepted images.
- **D.** The 9/67 KSDD2 portfolio test exceedances found by WP2 are all activation exceedances. After
  reweighting the test role to the qualification mixture, all 67 rates are ≤ α. This is consistent
  with the prevalence mismatch, but does not prove it.

## A. Tolerance sweep end-to-end (R2.4), KSDD2, 5 seeds

**Definitions.**
- *Ring at radius R:* the max-pool dilation of the mask by a (2R+1)² square, minus the mask.
- *Standard labels:* activation = (calls outside mask ∪ ring) / (pixels outside mask ∪ ring) > f0. This is
  the published rule. Recall always uses the unmodified mask, so evidence-loss labels do not depend on R.
- *Strict labels:* activation = (clean calls + ring calls) / clean pixels at R > f0. Ring calls count as
  activation. On clean images the ring is empty, so strict = standard there. The full analysis was rerun
  under strict labels: score models refitted, gates requalified.
- The radius also enters `secondary_peak` as the margin around the largest call component.

## B. Native-acquisition arm (R2.1), both domains

**Design.**
- The acquired reference image itself is the indication: no blur, no downsampling, no noise.
- Candidate actions:
  - raw;
  - Gaussian smoothing (3×3, σ = 0.8);
  - unsharp sharpening (5×5, σ = 1.0, amount 0.8). These are the pipeline's kernels, applied at native
    resolution.
  - the learned restorer applied to the native image. **The restorer was trained on bicubic-upsampled
    degraded → reference pairs**, so the native image is outside its training distribution.
- Unchanged from the published runs: the detector checkpoint, the stored pixel threshold, the feature
  definitions and the KSDD2 8-px ring. Forward consistency is measured against the native observation.
- Fitting and roles: score models are fitted on the native detector-val role (Carinthia-S
  class-balanced, KSDD2 MLE, as published). Tuning and hold-out come from the qualification split; the
  test role is descriptive. Targets: α = 0.15, δ = 0.10, r0 = 0.25, f0 = 0.002.
- **Policy family.** At native resolution, bilinear and bicubic coincide with raw. The family is
  therefore six policies: raw, smoothed, sharpened, learned, restoration pool {smoothed, sharpened,
  learned} and all-action pool. The portfolio allocation is δ/6. A δ/8 row (the eight-policy allocation
  kept) gives the same selections in all 10 domain-seed runs.
- **Important context.** Both detectors were trained on reference images (`train_detector` /
  `train_ksdd2_detector` use `batch["image"]`). The native raw image is therefore in the detector's
  training distribution.

## C. Degradation-family shift stress test (Carinthia-S; outside the guarantee)

**Design.**
- Fixed, as in the definitive Carinthia-S analysis:
  - checkpoints and detector thresholds;
  - score models (refitted from the frozen `detector_val_records.csv`; identical fits, confirmed by the
    nominal control);
  - the qualified portfolio gate (selected policy + certified threshold);
  - all 27 single-policy qualified gates (`tim_analysis/carinthia_s_seed{S}_floor0.25.json`).
- Re-rendered: only the test role (446 images: 430 positive, 16 clean). The published per-image severity
  level and the 2× bicubic downsampling are kept, and one operator component is replaced:
  - **motion:** linear PSF of length √12·σ_b, which has the same second moment along the motion
    direction. The angle is hashed per image.
  - **defocus:** uniform disk of radius 2σ_b (per-axis σ = σ_b).
  - Both keep the Gaussian noise σ_n. The kernel moments actually used are in
    `exp_c_kernel_moments.csv`: σ along motion 0.73/1.27/2.05 px and disk σ_x 0.70/1.23/2.03 px, vs
    Gaussian 0.59/1.18/1.99 px. Sub-pixel splatting adds about 0.1 px at the mild level.
  - **Poisson–Gaussian:** Gaussian blur; noise variance a·x + b with a = σ_n² and b = σ_n²/2. This equals
    σ_n² at mid-grey (x = 0.5).
  - **JPEG 30/50/70:** Gaussian blur; JPEG at a fixed quality replaces the additive noise.
- **The JPEG families are not severity-matched.** Their observations have higher PSNR than nominal
  (36.5–37.8 vs 32.2 dB, raw action), because they carry no additive noise.

## D. WP2 flag: KSDD2 portfolio test exceedances vs the prevalence mismatch

**Method.**
1. Each of the 67 qualified KSDD2 portfolio selections in the WP2 grid is re-run through the WP2 harness
   (`wp2_common.py`, imported read-only).
2. The accepted test set is split into positives and clean images.
3. The test activation rate is reweighted to the qualification-split mixture:
   - π_q = 109/604 = 0.180 and π_t = 110/1004 = 0.110;
   - w_pos = π_q/π_t = 1.647 and w_clean = (1−π_q)/(1−π_t) = 0.920.
4. Each recomputed test rate equals the WP2 stored value (assertion in the script).

## Limitations and notes

- All four experiments reuse data roles that were seen during development:
  - the KSDD2 qualification split and test role (E1, E4);
  - the Carinthia-S hold-out, after the ordering change (E5).
  Nothing here is confirmatory.
- **A.** α* is a descriptive diagnostic: it uses hold-out outcomes at every gate. Seeds share images,
  so the per-seed columns are repetitions, not independent samples.
- **B.**
  - Changing the policy family (six policies) is a design decision made here. The δ/8 sensitivity row
    gives the same selections.
  - The native Carinthia-S qualification says nothing about restoration. It reflects a detector applied
    to its training distribution, with a small clean subgroup (23 qualification and 16 test images).
- **C.** The matched-severity statement holds for the blur families (second moment) and for the
  Poisson–Gaussian variance at mid-grey only. It does not hold for JPEG. Only Carinthia-S was stressed,
  because KSDD2 has no qualified gates to hold fixed.
- **Disk use.** `ksdd2_tol*` and `native_*` hold record CSVs only; checkpoints were not copied. Logs are
  `log_*.txt`.
